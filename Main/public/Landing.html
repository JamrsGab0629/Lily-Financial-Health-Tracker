WHY FUZZY LOGIC

Money isn't just good or bad.

Traditional trackers force your finances into rigid boxes. Lily's fuzzy logic engine understands the in-between.

Traditional trackers
  • Fixed rules like "income above ₱30,000 is good"
  • Binary pass/fail spending labels
  • No sense of partial progress toward goals
  • Just numbers, no personalized coaching
VS
Lily's fuzzy engine
  • Gradual evaluation — 20% low, 70% medium, 10% high income
  • Understands moderate, borderline, and risky spending
  • Tracks goals as behind, near, or achieved
  • A cat companion that reacts to your real habits
